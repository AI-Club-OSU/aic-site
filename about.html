ABOUT AI CLUB

Building the future of AI at Oregon State.

Hands-on projects, technical workshops, mentorship, and industry networking — for beginners exploring AI for the first time and experienced developers building advanced systems.

WHO WE ARE

Learn, collaborate, and create.

The Oregon State University Artificial Intelligence Club brings together students passionate about artificial intelligence and machine learning through hands-on projects, technical workshops, mentorship, and industry networking.

We connect students with industry experts and researchers, support collaborative project teams, and showcase student-built AI applications. Through guest speakers from organizations including NVIDIA, Google, and NASA, members explore real-world applications of AI and learn from professionals working at the forefront of the field.

Our goal is to make AI accessible, practical, and impactful while strengthening OSU's growing AI ecosystem through student-led innovation and industry collaboration.

WHAT WE DO

Four ways to get involved

01 · BUILD

Hands-On Projects

Collaborate in structured project cohorts to develop real AI and machine learning applications, with opportunities to present your work at our end-of-term showcase.

02 · LEARN

Technical Workshops

Build practical skills through interactive workshops, beginner-friendly tutorials, and team-based technical sessions covering AI, machine learning, and related technologies.

03 · CONNECT

Industry Connections

Learn from guest speakers and researchers through presentations, discussions, and Q&A sessions featuring professionals from organizations such as NVIDIA, Google, and NASA.

04 · SHIP

Hackathons & Community Events

Turn ideas into working prototypes, collaborate across disciplines, and solve real-world problems through hackathons and larger community events.
